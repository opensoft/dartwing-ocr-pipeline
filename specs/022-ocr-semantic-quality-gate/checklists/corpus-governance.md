## Notes

- This checklist gates whether the corpus-governance requirements are precise enough to enforce the scored/calibration boundary algorithmically — both in the validator/evaluator and at PR review — without relying on reviewer judgment for ambiguous cases.
- The highest-risk gaps are: the undefined noncanonical-suffix recognition rule (CHK016–CHK017), the underspecified promotion checklist (CHK013), and the ambiguity about whether FR-027 applies uniformly to both corpus roots (CHK035).
- Baseline protection (CHK023–CHK028) is the hardest non-regression guarantee to enforce; SC-006 must be expressed as a machine-runnable comparison, not only a prose assertion, to be a credible release gate.

## Notes

- SC-010's commitment that "every active FR maps to at least one implementation task and at least one test — verified at `/speckit.tasks` and `/speckit.analyze` time" is the strongest test-coverage contract in this spec; every gap identified in this checklist that is not addressed in the spec before tasks are generated risks violating SC-010 at analyze time.
- The US2 Independent Test relies on `inv_024_hard_degraded_body` as its calibration fixture, but FR-026 and Assumptions state those files are not yet committed scored corpus data; the spec should clarify whether the fixture is committed as a test-only artifact before the gate tests are written.
- The determinism requirement (FR-014, SC-007) intersects with every other gate check; a single dedicated test that runs all gate logic twice and performs byte-identical comparison would provide stronger coverage than per-check determinism assertions spread across multiple test functions.
